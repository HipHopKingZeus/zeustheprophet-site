About Zeus The Prophet

Zaher Neket, known as Zeus The Prophet, is a Lebanese Australian hip hop artist. Beirut born and Melbourne raised, his journey runs through hip hop, faith, creativity, conscious lyricism and independent music.

He writes his own lyrics, records his own voice, hosts and produces The Zeus Podcast, and builds his work through I.Quality Online, with his faith in Jesus Christ first and foremost.
